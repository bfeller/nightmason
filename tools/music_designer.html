Arrangement

Roll: click to add a note and drag right to lengthen it. Drag a note to move it, or drag its right edge to resize. Right-click or double-click deletes; Del deletes the selected note. Click the keys on the left to hear a pitch.
Keyboard: Z–M and Q–U play the instrument like a piano (two octaves). With step record on, they also write notes at the cursor and move it on. Space plays the pattern, Shift+Space the song.
